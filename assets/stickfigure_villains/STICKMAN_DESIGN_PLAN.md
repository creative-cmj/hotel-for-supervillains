# Actual 3D stick-man villains — new primary plan

This plan supersedes `DESIGN_PLAN.md` and the four earlier cartoon-bodied prototypes. Those `.blend` and `.glb` files remain recoverable, but **they do not meet the new stick-man direction** and are not a quality baseline. The user's new 30-character lineup image is the primary appearance reference; where its shapes or colors disagree with an older plan row, the image wins. Queuejack has been updated below. The other rows are working notes awaiting character-by-character reconciliation. Heights include the feature at rest and are approximate metres against a 2.2 m hotel manager.

## One shared visual and technical language

Each ordinary body is a **large simple head, one narrow central rod, thin two-segment arms and legs, tiny joints/hands/feet, and dot eyes with a line mouth**. No human chest or hip masses, muscles, elaborate clothes, or chunky shoes. At roughly 1.8 m tall, target a 0.28–0.38 m head, 0.08–0.12 m body rod, and 0.035–0.06 m limbs; exceptions must reinforce the one huge trait. Sticks visibly meet at joints, and feet meet the floor. A character with a giant feature must *still* read as a stick person.

The shared rig uses root, body, neck, head, paired upper/lower arms and hands, paired upper/lower legs and feet. Feature bones are added only when needed. All guests need Idle, Walk, FastWalk, Turn, Talk, Listen, SitDown, SittingIdle, StandUp, HoldItem, GiveItem, ReceiveItem, Happy, Angry, Confused, Surprised, Impatient, plus one feature clip with personal timing. Target 1–3k triangles, two or three flat materials, no texture unless clearly needed; confirm actual counts after export. GLBs are Y-up, metric, with ground origins. Navigation, queuing, conversations, elevator travel, item exchange, and collision are gameplay integration tasks, not model-file behavior.

## Duplicate-trait and production check

The 30 giant traits occupy distinct silhouette regions: tail, carried mass, collar, side flag, forearm, neck, nozzle head, side frame, curtain, clock head, rook head, torso arrow, hands, leg spring, chest fan, rounded wings, jagged halo, shoulder triangles, cable arms, cushion body, page cape, thin profile, pointed wings, loop arm, crate head, finger, hat, wide body beam, grid backpiece, and lamp head. Mothball and Airmail differ in oval versus pointed wing shapes. Clockwise Clyde, Crateface, and Nightlight have circle, cube, and cone heads. External hallway/room props in character jokes are not required to play the self-contained feature clip.
