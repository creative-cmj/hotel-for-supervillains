# Simple villain roster — design lock for production

This plan uses the original 30 guest names and story hooks from the project's previous `SOURCE_ROSTER.md` (preserved on `backup/pre-stickfigure-roster`). It defines a **new** visual system; none of the deleted models is reused. Heights include the defining trait at rest, in metres. The playable manager is about 2.2 m tall. Faces use large eye shapes, two brows and one readable mouth. Three flat/matte colors per character are preferred; emissive material is reserved for Nightlight and tiny effect cues. All silhouettes must fit guest doors and the lift in their ordinary pose.

Shared construction: a continuous tapered torso-to-hip shape, tapered limbs with shaped elbow/knee transitions, mitten-like hands with a thumb notch, oversized but grounded shoes, and a neck socket that overlaps the torso and head. Feature geometry may be separate when it has its own pivot, but should look deliberately attached. Standard rig: root, pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head, paired upper/lower arms and hands, paired thighs/shins and feet. Extra controls only for the marked feature. Core animation timing is reused, but amplitude, speed, posture, and pauses change per guest.

## Duplicate-trait review

The primary visual jokes span tail/strip, sack, collar, torso patch, forearm, neck, nozzle head, split torso, curtain, clock head, rook crown, arrow torso, hands, spring leg, chest fan, wings, teleport trail, shoulder napkins, cable arms, cushion torso, page cape, thin profile, plane wings, rewind arm, crate head, pointing hand, hat, wide back, grid backpiece, and lamp head. The two winged guests differ: Mothball has rounded fuzzy wings, Airmail has sharp folded-paper wings. The three head-feature guests (Clockwise Clyde, Crateface, Nightlight) have distinct circle, cube, and cone silhouettes. Queuejack's paper tail is not a cape. Nightlight is the only always-emissive material.

## Production gate

The shared test figure and Characters 01 Queuejack, 02 Knickknack, and 03 Mrs Mute have passed front, three-quarter, side, back, gray, silhouette, floor contact, hotel scale, sampled animation, and GLB round-trip checks. Character 04 is next; 04–30 are not modeled. Each character must repeat those checks before moving on. The finished character should be small enough for a browser NPC; target roughly 2–8k triangles, 1–3 materials, and no texture larger than 1k unless a visual check justifies it. The first three each use five lightweight flat materials to keep their faces, bodies, footwear/props, and accent details individually readable.
