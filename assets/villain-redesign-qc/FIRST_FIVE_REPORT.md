# First Five Villain Redesigns

This is the first quality batch requested in the 30-villain redesign brief. The five weakest original silhouettes were selected because they read as ordinary, lightly varied humanoids at gameplay distance. The remaining 25 were deliberately left for later batches until this visual standard is accepted.

| Character | Before triangles | After triangles | Final dimensions (m) | Strong read |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Mister Monday | 2,136 | 4,276 | 1.598 × 1.910 × 1.160 | Slumped round hoodie, giant slippers, coffee mug |
| The Landlord | 1,792 | 3,080 | 1.721 × 2.024 × 1.286 | Square cardigan wall, tiny legs, keys and contract stack |
| The Auditor | 3,412 | 3,496 | 1.101 × 2.601 × 0.610 | Razor-thin ledger silhouette, pen shoulders, calculator |
| Doctor Oops | 2,256 | 4,272 | 1.342 × 1.955 × 0.971 | Oversized head, mismatched goggles, broken drone and mechanical arm |
| Agent Awkward | 2,068 | 2,416 | 1.448 × 2.460 × 1.056 | Tapered trench coat, bent nervous pose, huge briefcase |

## Inspection performed

- Front, back, left, right, high, and low views captured for every character.
- Face and hand close-ups captured for every character.
- Pure black silhouette comparison captured individually and as a five-character lineup.
- Automated joint-gap checks cover the head, neck, torso, hips, arms, hands, legs, and feet.
- Each character was spawned at gameplay scale inside its hotel location and checked against floors, ceilings, furniture, doors, and interaction prompts.
- All five remain below the project limit of 10,000 triangles per character.

The game currently supports idle movement, turning, and looking toward the player. Full walk, sit, laugh, carry, and reaction animation sets require the later rig-and-animation phase and are not represented as completed here.
